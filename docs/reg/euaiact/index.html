EU AI Act - Registration Database Obligations (Art. 49 and Art. 60)

Namespace: https://w3id.org/sbom/reg/euaiact/  |  euaiact.ttl |  v0.1.0

SKOS concept scheme modelling the minimum information elements required by EU Regulation 2024/1689 (EU AI Act) for: - Article 49 / Annex VIII: Market registration of high-risk AI systems - Article 60 / Annex IX: Real-world testing registration Source: Official Journal version, 13 June 2024 (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689). Text verified at https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/annex/8/ https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/annex/9/ https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/60/ Annex VIII structure (final regulation): Section A Provider, high-risk AI systems (Art. 49(1)) - 13 items Section B Provider, NOT-high-risk AI systems (Art. 49(2)) - 9 items Section C Deployer (Art. 49(3)) - 5 items Annex IX structure (final regulation): Real-world testing registration (Art. 60) - 5 items Two-layer structure ------------------- Layer 1 - Classification elements (euaiact:classification): Information needed to answer the decision questions determining WHETHER registration is required and WHICH path applies. Source: flowchart eu-ai-db-reg-flowchart (Art.49(1)-(5)) and Art.60(4)(c). Layer 2 - Registration form elements: euaiact:section-a Annex VIII Sec.A Provider, high-risk (Art.49(1)) euaiact:section-b Annex VIII Sec.B Provider, not-high-risk (Art.49(2)) euaiact:section-c Annex VIII Sec.C Deployer (Art.49(3)) euaiact:annex-ix Annex IX Real-world testing (Art.60) Art.49 registration paths (from flowchart): Q1: In Annex III? No => no registration Q1: Yes Q2: Annex III Point 2? Yes => national registration (not EU database) Q2: No Q3: Not-high-risk per Art.6(3)? Yes => EU database, Section B (lighter set) Q3: No (still high-risk) Q4: Annex III Pts 1/6/7 law enforcement/migration/asylum/border? Yes => EU database NON-PUBLIC, Section A (partial) [Sec.A item 12 instructions-for-use excluded] No Q5: Deployer is public authority / EU agency? No => EU database PUBLIC, Section A only Yes => EU database PUBLIC, Section A + Section C Art.60 testing registration paths (Art.60(4)(c)): Annex III pts 1/6/7 law enforcement/migration/asylum/border => non-public section, Annex IX pts 1,2,3,5 only Annex III pt.2 (safety components of harmonised products) => Art.49(5) national registration process All other Annex III systems => public EU database, full Annex IX (pts 1-5)

annex-ixAnnex IX - Real-world testing registration (Art. 60)Information to be submitted and kept up to date with regard to testing in real world conditions, registered in accordance with Article 60. Applies to providers or prospective providers of Annex III high-risk AI systems conducting testing outside AI regulatory sandboxes. Source: Annex IX, Regulation (EU) 2024/1689. (Registration under Art.60 is required before testing begins (Art.60(4)(c)). The system need not yet be placed on the market -- providers and prospective providers may register at any time before market placement (Art.60(2)). Entry into force: 2 August 2026.)
classificationRegistration eligibility classificationThe set of information elements needed to determine whether a system must register in the EU database and which Annex VIII section applies. Corresponds to the five decision questions in the Art. 49 flowchart (see eu-ai-db-reg-flowchart diagram).
q1-annex-iii-listingQ1: Annex III listingExternalConstraintWhether the AI system is listed in Annex III (Art. 49(1)) and, if so, which of the 8 points it falls under. If NO: no registration required. If YES: proceed to Q2. Annex III points: (1) Biometric identification and categorisation of natural persons (2) Safety components of products covered by Annex I harmonisation legislation (3) Education and vocational training (4) Employment, workers management and access to self-employment (5) Access to and enjoyment of essential private/public services and benefits (6) Law enforcement (7) Migration, asylum and border control management (8) Administration of justice and democratic processes
q2-annex-iii-point2Q2: Annex III Point 2 flag (safety component of harmonised product)ExternalConstraintWhether the AI system is specifically in Annex III Point 2: a safety component of a product covered by EU harmonisation legislation listed in Annex I (Art. 49(5)). If YES: national-level registration only -- EU database not required. If NO: proceed to Q3. (Annex I products include: machinery, medical devices, in-vitro diagnostics, civil aviation safety, rail systems, motor vehicles, agricultural tractors, marine equipment, lifts, pressure equipment, radio equipment.)
q3-art6-3-assessmentQ3: Not-high-risk determination under Art.6(3)ExternalConstraintWhether the provider has determined the AI system is NOT high-risk based on the conditions in Article 6(3). If YES (not high-risk): register in EU database under Annex VIII Section B. If NO (high-risk): proceed to Q4. Providers who use Art.6(3) must document their assessment before placing the system on the market and register under Art. 49(2). Section B item 6 requires identifying the specific Art.6(3) condition applied.
q4-law-enforcement-categoryQ4: Law enforcement / migration / asylum / border category flagExternalConstraintWhether the AI system falls under Annex III points 1, 6, or 7 specifically in the areas of law enforcement, migration, asylum and border control management (Art. 49(4)). If YES: EU database registration is in the NON-PUBLIC section; additionally, Annex VIII Section A item 12 (electronic instructions for use) is NOT required per the explicit exception in Sec.A item 12.
q5-deployer-typeQ5: Deployer is public authority or EU institution/agencyExternalConstraintWhether the deploying entity is a public authority or an EU institution, agency or body (Art. 49(3)). If YES: the deployer must register under Annex VIII Section C in addition to the provider's Section A registration. If NO: only the provider's Section A registration is required.

Source: Annex VIII Section B, Regulation (EU) 2024/1689.

Required when the deployer is a public authority or EU institution/agency/body. Source: Annex VIII Section C, Regulation (EU) 2024/1689.
