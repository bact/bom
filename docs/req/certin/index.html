India CERT-In SBOM & AIBOM Guidelines v2.0 Mapping

Namespace: https://w3id.org/bom/req/certin/  |  certin.ttl |  v0.1.0

SKOS concept scheme for the Indian Computer Emergency Response Team (CERT-In) Technical Guidelines on Software Bill of Materials (SBOM) and Artificial Intelligence Bill of Materials (AIBOM) Version 2.0 (Dated 09.07.2025). Covers 21 SBOM Data Fields (Chapter 4, Table 5) and 19 AIBOM Elements (Chapter 9, Table 10).
